Project Overview

Redesign the entire corporate website for a premium global LED lighting manufacturer. The objective is to create a world-class B2B website that reflects innovation, engineering excellence, manufacturing capability, and global trust.

The final website should feel comparable in quality to leading international lighting and industrial manufacturers, while maintaining a completely original visual identity, layout, and content structure. Do not copy the design, wording, or layout of any existing company.

Primary Goal

Create a website that communicates:

Premium Brand Identity
Advanced Manufacturing
Engineering Excellence
Global Export Capability
Product Innovation
Quality Assurance
Reliable Delivery
Premium After-Sales Service
Long-Term Customer Partnerships

Every page should reinforce these core messages consistently.

Research & Content Consistency

Before generating the design:

Analyze the entire existing website and all provided content.
Identify duplicate, outdated, or conflicting information.
Consolidate repeated content into a single clear section.
Ensure every statistic, feature, and claim remains consistent across all pages.
Use the same terminology, product naming, and brand voice throughout the website.
Remove unnecessary text and replace it with concise, premium copy.

Never invent certifications, export countries, manufacturing capacities, awards, client names, or technical specifications unless explicitly provided.

Content Guidelines
Keep headlines short and impactful.
Limit body text to 2–4 concise sentences per section.
Avoid repeating the same information on multiple pages.
Use clear hierarchy with consistent headings.
Ensure all statistics, claims, and terminology are consistent across the website.
